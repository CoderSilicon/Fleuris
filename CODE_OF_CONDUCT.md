# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want fleurıs to be a welcoming, respectful, and helpful project for everyone. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to take part in a way that makes others feel safe and included.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and respectful in words and actions.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions, and give constructive feedback.
- Respect different experiences, perspectives, and levels of knowledge.
- Focus on what is best for the people using and improving the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help make things right.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, demeaning, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing someone else’s private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other community interactions related to fleurıs. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers through a private channel available for this repository (for example, a private security/contact method provided by the maintainers). Reports will be handled with care, discretion, and respect for everyone involved.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take any action they consider appropriate in response to behavior that violates these expectations, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.
